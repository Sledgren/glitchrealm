Changelog · v7

What just
got better

Sync
Audio / MIDI Timing Engine
Direct clock sync using currentTime × tpb × (bpm/60) — same formula a DAW uses. No drift, no ratio math, no padding bugs.
Depth
3D Perspective System
Full pitch grid compression toward the horizon. The whole arrangement converges — not just note size. Dual-axis convergence creates a real tunnel effect.
Visual
Note Rendering Quality
5-layer note rendering — halo, body gradient, center highlight line, depth scale, and inactive fade. Every note looks lit from within.
Presets
13 Full Colorways
Aurora Ultra, Miami Vice, Neochrome, Cold Glass, Arcade Fire and 8 more. Each sets background, bloom, particle color, and all track colors in one click.
FX
Particles & Bloom
Stars animate from page open. Bloom intensity, count, size, speed, and color all independently controlled. Ripple rings on every active note.
Export
Video Bounce
Full-screen bounce overlay with live preview and progress bar. Exports WebM — plays in Chrome, Firefox, Android, Windows. MP4 option available.
