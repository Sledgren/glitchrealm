Changelog · v7 · 2026

What just got better.

Sync

Audio / MIDI Timing

Direct clock sync — same formula a DAW uses. No drift, no ratio math, no padding bugs. Closest to perfect we've been.

Depth

3D Perspective System

Full pitch grid compression toward the horizon. The whole arrangement converges — dual-axis creates a real tunnel effect.

Visual

Note Rendering

5-layer note rendering — halo, body gradient, center highlight line, depth scale, inactive fade. Every note lit from within.

Presets

13 Full Colorways

Aurora Ultra, Miami Vice, Neochrome, Cold Glass and 9 more. Background, bloom, particles, and all track colors in one click.

FX

Particles & Bloom

Stars animate from page open. Bloom, count, size, speed, color all independent. Ripple rings pulse on every active note.

Export

Video Bounce

Full-screen bounce overlay with live preview and progress bar. WebM output — Chrome, Firefox, Android, Windows.
