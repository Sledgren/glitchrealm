GR4600 — online groovebox & drum machine

GR4600 is a free online groovebox and drum machine that runs entirely in your web browser — nothing to install. Build beats on the step sequencer, lock the feel with the shuffle & swing engine, drop your own loops into the sampler, stack drum kits, and rough things up with lo-fi, vintage and bitcrush FX. Mix every track with EQ and compression, play basslines on the keyboard, then export to WAV (stems, master, or a live take).

Features

  • ▸ Step sequencer
  • ▸ Shuffle & swing groove
  • ▸ Drum synthesis (Web Audio)
  • ▸ Sampler & slicing
  • ▸ Drum kits / DRUMS BANK
  • ▸ Lo-fi & vintage FX
  • ▸ Mixer · EQ · compression
  • ▸ Bass keyboard
  • ▸ WAV export

FAQ

Is GR4600 free? Yes — it's 100% free and runs in your browser, no account and no download required.

Do I need to install anything? No. It's a web app built on the Web Audio API — open the page and start making beats.

UPDATE
  • ·Trim editor on every pad — right-click (or long-press on touch) any pad to open a zoomable waveform editor: drag the edges to cut start/end, drag the top corners for attack / decay. Wheel to zoom, drag the background to pan, release to preview. Works on drum pads, sampler slices and custom pads.
  • ·Drum trim & envelope — kick, snare, hats… now each have their own trim + attack/decay, kept in sync with the fader corner dots. Applied in playback, WAV export and saved with your slots.
  • ·⧉ Copy pad — the pad menu now has a COPY button: pick any destination pad across all 4 banks to duplicate a sound.
  • ·⌁ NOTE mode — new per-pad gate toggle: NOTE ON = the pad plays while held and stops on release (sustained samples, bass lines). NOTE OFF = classic one-shot.
  • ·Sampler waveform zoom — + / − buttons (or mouse wheel) in the sampler toolbar zoom in up to 1/4096th of the buffer for precise slice placement.
  • ·⊘ DRUMS — remove drums — new sampler button: harmonic/percussive separation (HPSS, pure JS, offline) strips the beat from any loaded sample so you can keep the melody/bass/vocals and play your own drums over it. Undoable in one click.
  • ·Touch fix — opening the pad menu with a long-press no longer fires the action twice.
  • ·Full MIDI controller support — pads, piano and sampler all respond to MIDI notes with real velocity. Works with any USB/MIDI keyboard or pad controller.
  • ·MPC mode — toggle between MPC layout (Akai C0 = pad 1, base 24) and piano layout (C4 = pad 1, base 60). Both ranges are accepted simultaneously — no mode switch needed when using both at once.
  • ·MIDI transport controls — Start / Continue → play, Stop → stop. Double-tap Stop (under 400 ms) cuts all sounds instantly. MIDI Clock ignored to avoid spam.
  • ·Auto-reconnect & watchdog — hotplug detection every 2 s, automatic permission re-request every 5 s if all devices disappear. MIDI stays live even after cable pull.
  • ·Latency compensation — audio is triggered retroactively by the JS event-loop delay (~5–15 ms) so drum hits land on the exact MIDI timestamp, not after.
  • ·Master FX rack — 16 studio effects on the master bus — reverb, delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, lo-fi, crusher, tape echo, vinyl sim, grain and more. Stack up to 4 at once.
  • ·Health monitor — silent background watchdog: auto-detects and recovers AudioContext freezes, scheduler stalls, and audio node crashes without interrupting your session.
  • ·New COMP / MASTER icons — EQ, compressor, tape, widener, limiter and LUFS sections now have dedicated SVG panel icons.
  • ·New GR4600 identity — Glitchrealm branding now appears across the interface.
  • ·Editable sequencer — click a SEQ block to edit it live (the wheel turns red), duplicate it into an independent variation.
  • ·Variable grid resolution — 1/16, 1/32 and triplets (1/8T, 1/16T). Right-click a step to double it (roll ×2/×3/×4).
  • ·Reworked TIME CORRECT — accessible straight from the screen, next to the BPM.
  • ·Tape machine — magnetic tape emulation (Ampex ATR-102) added to the MASTER, with INPUT / OUTPUT / BYPASS.
  • ·Reworked WAV export — offline rendering: perfect timing, stems + master exported in a single click.
  • ·Fixes — sampler pads (slice count) and mute/solo stuck between blocks.
